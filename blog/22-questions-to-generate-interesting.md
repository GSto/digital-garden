1.  What appears disorganized but is, in reality, a structured phenomenon?
    
2.  What appears to be a structured phenomenon, but in reality, it is disorganized?
    
3.  What seems to be individual but is holistic?
    
4.  What seems to be holistic but is individual?
    
5.  What happens locally but shows up globally?
    
6.  What happens globally but shows up locally?
    
7.  What changing thing appears to be stable?
    
8.  What stable thing appears to be changing?
    
9.  What seems efficient but is actually inefficient?
    
10.  What seems inefficient but is actually efficient?
     
11.  What strikes you as negative but is actually positive?
     
12.  What strikes you as positive bit is actually negative?
     
13.  What feels related, but is disconnected?
     
14.  What feels disconnected, that is related?
     
15.  What things seems like that can co-exist, that cannot?
     
16.  What things seem like they cannot co-exist, that can?
     
17.  What seems to be a positive correlation that’s actually a negative one?
     
18.  What seems to be a negative correlation that’s actually a positive one?
     
19.  What are two things that present as similar that are actually opposites?
     
20.  What are things that present as opposites that are actually similar?
     
21.  What effects seem like causation that are independent?
     
22.  What effects seem independent, but have a chain of causation?
     

Inspired by Murray Davis's [That's Interesting](https://proseminarcrossnationalstudies.files.wordpress.com/2009/11/thatsinteresting_1971.pdf)

Disclaimer: I never said the ideas would be correct, useful, or good. I said they would be *interesting.*
